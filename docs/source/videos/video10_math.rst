Video 10: How to Do Math in Arduino Programs
======================================================

This lesson covers mathematical operations in Arduino sketches.

* **Arithmetic operators:** Demonstrates addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
* **Order of operations:** Explains how expressions are evaluated.
* **Scaling values:** Shows how to scale input and output values.
* **Practical calculations:** Demonstrates math usage in sketches.
