Video 24: How to Use Interrupts
==========================================================

This lesson introduces hardware interrupts and their behavior.

* **Interrupt concept:** Explains how interrupts differ from polling.
* **Interrupt pins:** Identifies pins that support interrupts.
* **Trigger modes:** Demonstrates rising, falling, and change triggers.
* **ISR functions:** Shows how interrupt service routines are defined.
